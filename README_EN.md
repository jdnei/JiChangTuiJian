## What You Should Know Before Choosing a Service

* Generally speaking, relay/transit services are more recommended than direct-connection services. Transit services usually have larger bandwidth capacity, while dedicated-line services mainly offer lower latency and avoid certain routing restrictions, making everyday use smoother and more comfortable. In the end, service quality largely depends on how much infrastructure the provider is willing to invest in. Speed depends mainly on the provider's total bandwidth capacity. Stability depends on the quality of the provider's upstream network resources and routes. Streaming and regional-content unlocking depend on the quality of the provider's exit nodes and unlocking services.
* For transit services, the protocol itself is usually not the most important factor. The stability and bandwidth of the relay servers matter more.
* Having backup services is extremely important. If you frequently need unrestricted internet access, you should not rely on only one provider. It is better to have several backup services.
