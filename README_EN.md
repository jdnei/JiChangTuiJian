## Must Read Before Choosing a VPN Service
<details>
<summary><strong>Click to expand</strong></summary>

- Compared with direct-connect services, relay services are generally more recommended. Relay services usually have higher bandwidth, while dedicated lines mainly offer lower latency and a smoother experience in daily use. Speed depends largely on the service’s total bandwidth. Stability depends on the quality of the provider’s resources, especially upstream routes, while content unlocking depends on the quality of exit nodes and unlocking partners. High bandwidth and stability matter most.
- For relay services, the protocol is clearly not the most important factor; the stability and bandwidth of the relay servers matter more.
- Having backups is especially important. If you frequently need circumvention access, you should not rely on only one service and should keep several backup services.
